Chapter 3

How I work

Three rules. I learned each one the hard way.

1. Read all of it

I read the full log, the full design and the full history before I act. The cause is often in the part that nobody read.

2. Change the ending

To know what will fail is not enough. I fix it, so the same incident does not return.

3. Do not read alone

A system is safer when more than one person understands it. I share what I know and I ask for review.
